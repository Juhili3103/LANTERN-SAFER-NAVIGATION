Every street segment gets a safety penalty recalculated for the exact hour you select — not one fixed score:

lighting — unlit segments are penalized only after dark (8pm–6am); irrelevant in daylight.
foot traffic — each street type has a typical "how busy is this normally" curve by hour; empty-at-night streets score worse after dark even with zero reports.
incidents — crowd reports are weighted by how close their reported hour is to yours, so a string of 11pm reports barely affects a 9am walk, and vice versa.

The "fastest" route always minimizes distance. The "recommended" route minimizes distance × (1 + 2.5 × penalty) for your selected hour — so the two routes can diverge completely between 6pm and 11pm on the same street grid.

Reports you submit are saved to the database when one is connected, and are added on top of the synthetic baseline reports.
Demo dataset — the street grid, streetlight coverage and baseline incident reports are synthetic, for illustration. Not a real map. Reports you submit are stored only if a database is connected.
